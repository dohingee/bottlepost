Bottlepost lets you write anonymous letters to strangers somewhere in the world, like setting a message in a bottle out to sea.

■ Send a bottle
Pick who may receive it (anyone, women or men) and your letter drifts to one person at random. When they reply, you've made a connection.

■ One letter at a time
Letters take turns, one each. While you wait for a reply, scroll back and reread your letters.

■ Start without signing up
No login needed — just your gender and birth year. Link Sign in with Apple to restore your conversations on a new iPhone.

■ Different languages welcome
Translate letters right on your device. The app is available in English, Korean, Japanese, Spanish, Portuguese, Indonesian, Vietnamese and Thai.

■ Your letters stay on your iPhone
We don't keep letter text on our servers. A letter is deleted from the relay as soon as it is delivered; conversations live only on your iPhone (and, if you choose, an encrypted backup in your own iCloud).

■ Stay safe
Contact details and links are blocked automatically. Report or block any letter, leave a conversation or delete it at any time. Reported letters are reviewed and acted on under our rules.

For adults 18 and over only.
