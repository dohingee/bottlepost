Bottlepost lets adults (18+) send one anonymous letter to a random adult. A conversation only continues if the recipient replies, and each side must wait for the other's reply (turn-taking). It is not a real-time chat and has no public feed: letters are limited per day and each letter is delivered once, to one person.

Guideline 1.2 (user-generated content), where to find each precaution:
1. Age rating 18+; birth year checked at onboarding and on the server; Apple's Declared Age Range on iOS 26.
2. Terms (EULA) with zero tolerance: the onboarding screen shows "Zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users..." above the required "I agree to the Terms of Service" checkbox. Terms section 6-7: https://bottlepost.web.app/terms-en
3. Filtering: before a letter is sent, the server blocks links, phone numbers, e-mail addresses, messenger IDs and crypto addresses; reported letters are checked by a rules engine and a moderation model.
4. Flag content: every received letter shows a "Report" button under it (also in the conversation's ... menu and by long-pressing a conversation in the list).
5. Block users: every received letter shows a "Block" button (same places as Report). Reporting also blocks the sender.
6. Remove content immediately: reporting or blocking removes the conversation from the user's screen at once; any conversation can also be deleted (long-press > Delete conversation, or ... > Delete).
7. We review reports within 24 hours. Confirmed violations: the content is removed (the sender's undelivered letters are deleted on the server) and the sender is suspended or permanently banned. Child-safety reports suspend the sender at once.
8. Contact in the app: Settings > Contact support (kimyeoware@gmail.com, shown on screen) and in the report form. Child safety: https://bottlepost.web.app/child-safety

No login is needed: onboarding asks only gender and birth year. Sign in with Apple (Settings) is optional and only restores the account and its encrypted iCloud backup on a new iPhone; it requests no name or e-mail. Account deletion: Settings > Delete account.

To receive a reply during review, send a letter choosing "Anyone": the operator's test account replies, usually within a few hours.

Ads: this version shows no ads and does not track (no App Tracking Transparency prompt). The ad SDK is included but switched off; ads will come in a later version with updated privacy details.
